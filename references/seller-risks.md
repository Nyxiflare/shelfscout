# Seller risk checklist

Go through these for every candidate. A single "high" can sink a product that looks great on margin.
Point the user to the official rule or regulation. Don't present this as legal or certification advice.

## Intellectual property
- **Trademarks:** is the product name, logo or a distinctive design someone's brand? Search the
  trademark database for the target country (USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO). "Inspired by [brand]" listings get
  removed and can lead to account suspension.
- **Design and utility patents:** clever mechanisms and unusual shapes are often patented. A search on
  Google Patents for the key feature is a cheap first check.
- **Licensed characters, sports teams, artwork:** need a licence. Never suggest selling them without one.

## Restricted or gated categories
- Marketplaces restrict or require approval for categories like supplements, cosmetics, food, medical
  devices, children's products, jewellery, and some brands. Check the marketplace's category approval
  pages before investing.

## Safety and compliance (examples, by region)
- **Electronics:** FCC (US), CE/UKCA (EU/UK), and battery transport rules (UN38.3) for lithium batteries.
- **Children's products:** CPSIA and a Children's Product Certificate (US), EN 71 (EU). Small parts and
  magnets are high risk.
- **Food-contact items:** FDA (US) and LFGB or EU 10/2011 (EU).
- **Cosmetics and supplements:** labelling and ingredient rules. Claims like "cures" or "treats" are a
  red flag.
- **Electrical / mains-powered:** UL/ETL listing is expected by many US buyers and some marketplaces.

## Operational risks
| Risk | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Fragile, heavy or oversized | High fulfilment fees and damage returns |
| Sizing (clothing, shoes, rings) | High return rates; budget `--returns-pct 15`+ |
| Seasonal | Cash tied up in stock outside the season |
| Commodity with many identical sellers | A price war that erases margin |
| Fast-moving fads | Demand can vanish before stock arrives |
| Single supplier, no samples | Quality risk; always order samples first |
| Price under ~$15 | Fixed fees eat the margin; hard to fund ads |

## Supplier checks (for sourcing from marketplaces like Alibaba or 1688)
- Years in business, verified/inspected status, response rate, and real factory vs. trading company.
- Ask for samples, certificates in your name (not just theirs), and lead time in writing.
- Pay through the platform's trade assurance where it exists. Never pay unknown suppliers by wire alone.

## Margin rules of thumb (state them as rules of thumb)
- Under 15% net margin after ads and returns: too thin to survive a price drop or fee increase.
- Selling price at least about 3× the landed product cost is a common starting target for marketplace
  products with ads.
- Check fees on the marketplace's current fee page for the category. `SS fees` shows the presets and
  when they were checked.
